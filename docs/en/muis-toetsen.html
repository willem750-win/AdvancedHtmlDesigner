Mouse & keys

Overview of what you can do in the designer with the mouse and the keyboard.

Mouse

ActionResult
Click a cellSelects the cell.
Drag over cellsSelects several cells (for example to merge or format them together).
Click a row or column headerSelects the whole row or column.
Drag the line between two headersChanges the width of that column or the height of that row.
Double-click a cellStarts editing the text of the cell.
Ctrl + dragMoves the whole table across the page.
Click a text blockSelects the block.
Drag a text blockMoves the block.
Drag the bottom-right cornerResizes the text block.
Double-click a text blockStarts editing the text of the block.
Click empty spaceClears the selection.

Keyboard in the designer

KeyResult
← → ↑ ↓To the previous/next cell.
Enter or F2Edits the selected cell or the selected text block.
DeleteDeletes the selected text block, or clears the text of the selected cell.
Ctrl+CCopies the selected cell(s).
Ctrl+VPastes into the selected cell.

While editing text

KeyResult
EnterNew line in the text.
Ctrl+EnterFinish editing and keep the text.
EscCancel editing; the old text stays.
Tip: clicking outside the edit box also finishes editing and keeps the text.
